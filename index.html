Core Problem Statement

Problem Statement Definition

"Novo Nordisk India lacks an automated data intelligence layer to understand tier-wise sales performance—leaving commercial teams reliant on manual Excel projections to decide which products to launch in Tier-2 vs Tier-1 cities, and how to allocate sales reps to capture revenue gaps."

Background & Context

  • Nationwide Network: Novo Nordisk operates across all of India, collecting extensive distributor, pharmacy, and hospital sales data.
  • Regional Execution Disparity: Product adoption varies significantly by market tier—Tier-1 Metros (NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru) show rapid GLP-1 adoption, while Tier-2 Growth Hubs (Lucknow, Jaipur, Coimbatore) represent a ₹285 Cr untapped revenue gap due to doctor coverage limits and distributor stock caps.
  • Manual Projections: Territory forecasting and sales targets are currently estimated manually in static spreadsheets rather than using real-time predictive machine learning.

Project Objectives

  • Automate Revenue Attribution: Instantly pinpoint underperforming cities and diagnose root causes (Doctor Coverage Gap vs. Stockist Credit Cap vs. Price Sensitivity).
  • Tier-Based Product Launch Matcher: Recommend the optimal rollout sequence (Tier-1 ➔ Tier-2 ➔ Tier-3) for pipeline therapies like Icodec once-weekly insulin and Wegovy.
  • Field Resource Simulator: Allow commercial leadership to simulate sales rep re-allocations to maximize 12-month revenue ROI.
